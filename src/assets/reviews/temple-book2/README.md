# Temple Book 2(A), 1882–1888: transcription review

Source: [Temple record](https://digital.library.temple.edu/digital/collection/p15037coll19/id/16226/rec/4). SCRC 50, Box 2, Folder 1; JHSSMZ202010000017.

All 290 scans have a page-inventory record. 58 grouped named lists, 1213 entries and 120 linked source images are prepared as an AI-assisted draft. Dates, repeated appearances and source-specific name forms remain separate. This is extraction of identified named lists, not a diplomatic transcription of all 290 pages. Full prose, speeches, individual narrative mentions, anonymous totals, expense warrants and attendance-table tick/dot cells are not transcribed. The latter remain visible in the table images.

Uncertain handwriting is flagged. All other readings remain provisional and require independent proofreading. The building-fund amounts for L. Mayer and raffle proceeds on scan 252 need checking, and the relationship to the written total on 253 is unresolved. No corrected total is invented. Meeting dates sometimes disagree between headings, margins, printed reports and board tables. The draft notes record observed discrepancies.

Cash gifts, loans, materials, awards and offices are distinguished. Repeated annual acknowledgments are generally counted once. The Clarence/Clarance Hart banner appears in both annual contexts; it is the same reported gift, explicitly noted. No personal identity matching is asserted.

- `page-inventory.csv`: all 290 scans and their disposition, with original links.
- `transcription.json`: draft readings grouped by list.
- `entries.csv` and `lists.csv`: new site records.
- `manifest.json`: source manifest.
- `list-checks.json`: counts by list.

Review status: named-list extraction complete for the identified rosters; independent proofreading and the explicitly excluded diplomatic transcription remain outstanding.
