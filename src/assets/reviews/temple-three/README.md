# Three Temple Hebrew Sunday School Society records

AI-assisted draft, not independently proofread. All source scans visually reviewed and inventoried for named lists. Full transcription of identified list entries, with narrative school leaders and appointments extracted into labelled lists; not a verbatim transcription of all prose or expense warrants. Abbreviations and source spelling variants retained without inferred personal identity links. Uncertain handwritten readings flagged. Parallel print/manuscript accounts of the same event combined. Membership rates, investment returns, book sales and salary payments are not personal donations. Legacy capital and later receipts may be related; do not sum them as independent new gifts.

482 scans inventoried; 120 lists; 2553 entries; 262 source images attached.

## Hebrew Sunday School Society: president’s report, 1881

7 scans. SCRC 50, Box 1, Folder 11; JHSSMZ202010000016. [Temple record](https://digital.library.temple.edu/digital/collection/p15037coll19/id/19743).

Temple catalogue Date field says 1861; both report versions are dated October 23, 1881. Scans 001–003 are a later retyping, 004–006 the manuscript parallel, 007 largely blank. Later annotation 6/11/40 is not the report date.

## Hebrew Sunday School Society: Book 3 minutes, 1888–1894

240 scans. SCRC 50, Box 2, Folder 9; JHSSMZ202010000023. [Temple record](https://digital.library.temple.edu/digital/collection/p15037coll19/id/17226).

Includes manuscript minutes, pasted annual reports, event programs and concluding annual board roll tables. Individual attendance marks in the final grid are not transcribed; roster names and meeting attendance in minutes are transcribed. Scan 007 includes an unrelated inverted clipping, excluded.

## Hebrew Sunday School Society: Book 5 minutes, 1900–1905

235 scans. SCRC 50, Box 3, Folder 10; JHSSMZ202010000029. [Temple record](https://digital.library.temple.edu/digital/collection/p15037coll19/id/20905).

Includes pasted annual reports for 1900–1905. Prize lists in the 1904 and 1905 reports refer respectively to 1903 and 1904 ceremonies. Some membership continuations are absent from the pasted report; no missing names invented.

The inventory records blank pages, pasted inserts, accounts and excluded non-list material. Named expense recipients are not converted into donors. Individual attendance-grid cells and full prose are available on the original scans rather than transcribed as list entries. These sources overlap annual reports elsewhere in the collection; records are source appearances, not unique people or aggregate donation totals.
