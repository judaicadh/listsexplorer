# NLI newspaper subscriber research

## Confirmed subscriber list
6 August 1880, no. 6, p. 8, column 1: “Danksagung.” Ten named subscriber entries who sent issue no. 3 to the publishers. Transcribed against the scan; Sisel Marsh marked probable. Further unnamed respondents excluded. No amounts printed.

https://www.nli.org.il/en/newspapers/diedeborah/1880/08/06/01/article/29/

Scan crop assembled without alteration from nine native-resolution 256-pixel viewer tiles; the downloaded tile manifest is retained in the working research directory. Crop corresponds to source-page x=115–690, y=2680–3275. No claim of complete NLI archive review.

## Named agent notices (research evidence, not imported as separate rosters)
16 October 1868, p. 4, “Notiz”: Julius I. Cohn, general agent for Israelite and Deborah at 222 East Broadway, New York; I. M. Rosenberg no longer their agent.
https://www.nli.org.il/en/newspapers/diedeborah/1868/10/16/01/article/12/

https://www.nli.org.il/en/newspapers/diedeborah/1868/11/13/01/article/13/

11 December 1874 “An die Abonnenten” asks unnamed collection agents to remit arrears; not a named agent list.

## Search scope
Targeted searches included Agenten, “unsere Agenten”, “unser Agent”, Quittungen, Agenten AND Deborah, and Abonnenten AND Namen. Selected promising hits read; many agent hits were commercial advertisements or unrelated literary text. Search results and OCR are discovery aids, not exhaustive page review.
