# NLI newspaper subscriber and agent research

## Named newspaper lists

- 11 July 1856, no. 47, scan 8, column 2: seven further orders for the proposed monthly European edition. The general annual price is not treated as an individual payment. Publication was conditional on 200 orders. Scan compared; no individual amounts or addresses printed.
  https://www.nli.org.il/en/newspapers/diedeborah/1856/07/11/01/article/9/
- 6 August 1880, no. 6, scan 8, column 1: “Danksagung.” Ten named subscriber entries who sent issue no. 3 to the publishers. Further unnamed respondents excluded. Sisel Marsh marked probable. No amounts printed.
  https://www.nli.org.il/en/newspapers/diedeborah/1880/08/06/01/article/29/
- 19 October 1883, no. 16, scan 8, column 2: five named subscribers who sent earlier numbers. Scan compared; difficult initials and given name marked probable. No amounts printed.
  https://www.nli.org.il/en/newspapers/diedeborah/1883/10/19/01/article/17/

  https://www.nli.org.il/en/newspapers/diedeborah/1868/10/16/01/article/12/

- Wm. Meyer: 11 December 1868 notice describes the agent recruiting subscribers in southern Iowa, then northern Missouri and part of Illinois. Signed Bloch & Co., 4 December. A 19 November 1869 notice authorizes him as travelling agent for all states and validates his receipts. OCR read; independent scan proofreading pending.
  https://www.nli.org.il/en/newspapers/diedeborah/1868/12/11/01/article/9/
  https://www.nli.org.il/en/newspapers/diedeborah/1869/11/19/01/article/9.1/

  https://www.nli.org.il/en/newspapers/diedeborah/1885/01/09/01/article/9/
- G. Deutsch, Hyde Park, Cincinnati, named for remittances in “Vorwärts, Deborah!”, 1 December 1901. This is a remittance contact, not an explicit formal agent appointment. Editorial says a travelling agent was too costly and asks existing subscribers to recruit readers.
  https://www.nli.org.il/en/newspapers/diedeborah/1901/12/01/01/article/2/

## Broader search coverage

The NLI archive advertises 1,947 issues and 11,766 pages with gaps. This is a targeted search pass, not a page-by-page transcription of that archive.

Result-title screening completed for:
- Abonnenten AND Namen: all 29 results.
- Abonnenten: all 151 ARTICLE and 57 ADVERTISEMENT results, with selected explicit notices and representative recurring columns read. The 498 TITLE SECTION results are largely repeated masthead terms and were not all read.
- Agentur: all 133 result titles; selected relevant notices and representative commercial advertisements read.
- Agenten AND Deborah: all 51 result titles; selected publisher notices and ambiguous hits read.
- Quittungen: all 36 result titles; selected receipt notices read. The 1869 agent receipt differs from a 1872 printer's advertisement and a 1857 grammar-book advertisement.
- Zeitungsgelder: no results.

Results overlap and must not be summed as unique documents. OCR misses and poor article segmentation mean short embedded lists can remain undiscovered. Title screening does not mean every article or repeated advertisement has been transcribed. The separate supplied-PDF review inventory retains its original scope.

Book subscribers for Rosenheim's poems (28 November 1862 and 9 January 1863) are separate leads, not newspaper subscribers. The April 1901 reader correspondence contains satire and must not be converted into a verified subscriber roster.
