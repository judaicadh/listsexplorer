# Constitution and incorporation papers

Temple catalogues this six-scan manuscript as Constitution and Articles of Incorporation, 1838. The preamble recalls establishment of the school in 1838; the court order on scan 004 dates the application May 10, 1858 and incorporation June 7, 1858, and the recorder endorsement on scan 005 is dated June 9, 1858. The signature block itself is undated. Do not treat these as proven 1838 founding signatures. All six scans visually reviewed; all 14 signatures transcribed in source order. AI-assisted draft, not independently proofread. Legal prose and marginal endorsements inventoried rather than fully transcribed. No donation amounts or personal identity matches inferred.
