# Temple Hebrew Sunday School Society, Book 6

AI-assisted draft, not independently proofread. All 239 source scans visually reviewed and inventoried for named lists. Identified list entries transcribed; not a verbatim transcription of narrative prose or expense warrants. Original abbreviations and spelling variants retained without inferred personal identity links. Uncertain handwriting flagged. Parallel print/manuscript accounts of the same event combined. Annual donation acknowledgments overlap meeting receipts and earlier reports; do not sum them as independent gifts. Salary payments, purchases, book sales and investment returns are not donations. Historical endowment capital is separate from current receipts.
