Transcription quality audit · 22 November 2026

All 974 lists and 103,194 entry name fields were screened for address fragments and narrative headings. This is a screening audit, not a visual proofread of every page. No flags does not mean a list is correct. Organization names containing street/place names can be legitimate.

At least 21 National Jewish Blue Book lists need a redo; Boston Combined Jewish Appeal pledges has the same confirmed segmentation problem. Two Hebrew Sunday School Society event lists need structural review because buildings/financial events are treated as participants. Executives has been corrected separately.
