# Temple annual report, 1915

[Original Temple record](https://digital.library.temple.edu/digital/collection/p15037coll19/id/21146/rec/20). SCRC 50, Box 4, Folder 1; JHSSMZ202010000030.

All 31 scans reviewed. 13 named lists, 513 structured entries and 24 source images. AI-assisted draft, not independently proofread. Full extraction of identified named lists, not diplomatic transcription of narrative prose, speeches or anonymous financial totals.

The graduate roll has 40 graduates and 20 post-graduates; narrative dates the anniversary June 7, 1914, despite the 1915 report cover. The award table has 84 entries, without a separate ceremony date. The staff register has 209 source appearances, including the same person serving in different capacities. School statistics distinguish admissions, class counts and roll counts.

Explicit source variants are retained: Stein/Stern in election/board lists, Lipman/Lippman in officer/committee headings, Michael S./D. Levy in prize offer/award. Prize cash values offered in the report are annotations, not invented treasury receipts. Books, medals and services have no invented prices or copy quantities. The named current receipts include an institutional appropriation and school collections as labelled sections.

The memorial register on scans 029–030 explicitly identifies the Federation of Jewish Charities. Its 58 entries and 4 special endowments retain original years and values in notes, not current donation fields. Some entries repeat historical registers in other reports; they are distinct source appearances, not newly received gifts.

`page-inventory.csv` records every scan, including blank pages and exclusions. `transcription.json`, `entries.csv` and `lists.csv` contain the draft readings and new site records. `manifest.json` preserves archive provenance.
