Address inventory and geocoding, 2026-11-22
470 address records. 225 are separately transcribed residence, office or unlabelled addresses from the scan-checked Executives list; 245 come from existing printed place/address fields and have not been newly scan-checked.
170 US queries submitted to US Census Public_AR_Current benchmark: 141 Match, 13 Tie, 16 No_Match. Every returned coordinate remains a candidate, with street-range precision. No historical building location is asserted. Query-only locality expansion is recorded in each address note. Source spelling is preserved, including unusual readings. Non-US addresses and records with insufficient/uncertain context remain unmapped.
The original query and response CSVs are retained here. Current records are downloadable at /data/addresses.csv; entries.csv is unchanged.
Method: https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/Geocoding_Services_API.html

Screening excluded three returned coordinates whose queries named buildings/rooms rather than an unambiguous street address. Those responses remain recorded as rejected; 138 candidates are displayed on the map.
