## Other material

- The 11 August 1871 PDF is an incomplete clipping of the already catalogued agents box; the JPG is the more complete witness. No extra list was created.
- The 22 August 1873 PDF contains the existing Anglo-Jewish Association Victorian Branch list; no extra copy was created.
- The Fiji notice of 30 June 1871 reports weekly services in Levuka and P. S. Solomon as reader. It contains no membership roster, donation list or agents list, so it was not turned into one.

## Corrections and uncertainty

- Three fold-obscured Sydney amounts were previously conjectured to reconcile the printed total. Their numeric values are now blank, with the earlier conjectures retained in notes for transparency. Names remain marked uncertain.
- Newspaper agents remain dated observations, with printed spellings, towns and addresses. They now appear on the Agents page, separately from entries forwarded on behalf of donors.
- The literary society acknowledgment records both B. S. Cohen and J. Harris as intermediaries; gifts are books of unspecified quantity and value.
