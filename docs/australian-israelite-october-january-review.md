# Australian Israelite: October 1871 to January 1872

Added 18 dated lists, 236 entries.

## Source decisions
- The two 5 January agent images overlap; one complete record uses the fuller crop (1).
- The two 13 October page 1 crops contain different notices and both are represented.
- The 20 October page 6 Brown’s Exchange Hotel advertisement names no roster, donors or agents. It is not converted into a list.
- Weekly agent appearances are preserved as dated evidence; no cash amounts or inferred payments are attached.
- The 27 October Sympathy Fund notice requests subscribers to forward money: these are promises. The 3 November notice says additional subscriptions received. No individual reconciliation between promises and later payments has been assumed.
- The lower 3 November section has a dark fold. Two names and one amount are unreadable; all rows are retained and lower-section readings are uncertain.
- The benefit society’s invested fund and fee schedule are not donations by its officers.
