# Australian Israelite: late 1872 batch

- Six dated newspaper-agent lists preserve the address change from 87 Harrington-street (6 December) to 158 Castlereagh-street (13 December).
- On 20 December the publisher of the Jewish Chronicle, 43 Finsbury Square, London, is added under England. No individual publisher identity is inferred.
- The 8 and 15 November ball notices repeat the same roster. One record retains both scans. D. Benjamin appears as both steward and committee member; both capacities remain. Admission prices are not donor amounts.
- The 18 October Day of Atonement acknowledgment retains two explicit second donations and explains the ditto references.
- Sermon, seat-allocation and almshouse-application notices beside the donation list are not treated as donor rosters.
