# Australian Israelite: April–October 1872 batch

## Source decisions
- Thirteen three-person newspaper-agent rosters are retained with their issue dates and addresses.
- The Persian Famine Relief Fund committee notice is repeated on 24 and 31 May. One 24-person record preserves both scans; repeated publication does not create a second set of appointments. Committee members are explicitly authorised receivers, not donors.
- The St. Kilda requisition has twelve signers. Their names repeated in the reply are not extra signatures. The recipient and respondent are recorded separately.
- The Stephen-street petition has 31 signatures. Occupations are transcribed in notes; estate agents and commission agents are not classified as financial intermediaries solely on that wording.
- The 26 April synagogue building list is explicitly cumulative. Earlier 1871 acknowledgments are cross-referenced; the two totals must not be added as independent gifts.
- The consecration acknowledgment is kept separate. Its two Joseph Cohen rows and anonymous Cash aggregate are retained as printed.
- Three office holders in the adjacent 26 April appointment notice are recorded separately from the donors. Other sermon and commercial notices are not converted into donor or member rosters.
